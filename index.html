Eleven Stars

Venus, the great star of the upper left, the whirlpool's eye — each one located individually, breathing and shimmering with its own phase and rhythm.

The Moon

The crescent at the upper right breathes on a slow 14-second cycle, its brightness pulsing in step with its warm golden halo.

The Flowing Sky

A twin-whirlpool flow field drives the brushwork of the whole sky, recreating the roll and curl of the night air Van Gogh painted.

The Cypress

The dark flame of a tree on the left sways in the wind — broad slow sweeps at the crown, quick tremors among the leaves, steady roots.

The Sleeping Village

Thirteen window lights flicker with a candlelight rhythm of two frequencies, warm yellow glows trembling softly in the dark.

Shooting Stars

A meteor crosses the sky once in a while — or you can summon one yourself at any moment by clicking the painting.

Every color is taken from the painting itself — the animation changes only brightness and position, never Van Gogh's palette.
